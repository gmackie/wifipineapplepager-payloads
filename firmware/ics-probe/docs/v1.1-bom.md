## Ordering Notes

- **ADUM3154** has long lead times. Order early or keep ADUM1401+ADUM1201 as backup (Option B).
- **AD5420** is the most expensive single part. Analog Devices often has free samples for prototyping — check [analog.com/samples](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/technical-tools/samples.html).
- **MAX14906** also available via Maxim/ADI samples.
- **W5500 module boards** from AliExpress are ~$2 and include the crystal + MagJack — much easier than bare W5500 for breadboard prototyping.
- **TSSOP-24 breakout adapters** are essential for breadboarding the MAX14906 and AD5420. Order a 5-pack.
- For the 3.3V LDO on the 24V rail: any standard AMS1117-3.3 or AP2112K-3.3 works. Check dropout voltage — at 24V input, dropout is irrelevant.
